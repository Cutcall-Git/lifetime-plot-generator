# A Very Lifetime TV Movie Plot Generator

A tiny, scientifically questionable generator for creating extremely plausible Lifetime-style TV movie plots.

The generator builds a protagonist, setting, family circumstances, male counterpart, animals, seasonal events, conflicts, and possible scenes from a declarative narrative ontology.

The basic idea is intentionally simple:

- **Representation creates probability.** Common outcomes occupy more positions in a selection pool or are represented at finer granularity.
- **Earlier selections expose later possibilities.** Autumn can expose harvest festivals and pumpkin patches; summer cannot.
- **Unavailable possibilities simply do not exist in the active pool.** The generator should not need sprawling conditional logic to assign them artificial zero probabilities.
- **Affiliations connect concepts.** A ranch can expose horses. Control issues can expose situations involving loss of control. A yellow Labrador can apparently solve almost anything.
- **The JavaScript should remain stupid.** Narrative complexity belongs in the ontology rather than in a giant procedural generator.

The goal is not to generate a complete screenplay. The generator produces a premise and a compatible spill of characters, circumstances, conflicts, and scene ideas that could plausibly belong to the same terrible television movie.

No AI is required.

Only fate, Christmas, unresolved family trauma, a small town, and possibly a horse.
